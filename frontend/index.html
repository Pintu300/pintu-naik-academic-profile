About

Parallel algorithms and heterogeneous computing

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu (IIT Jammu), India.

I study scalable parallel and distributed algorithms, with a focus on GPU-accelerated graph processing and maximum-flow computation. My work includes memory-efficient graph representations, architecture-aware optimization, and experimental performance analysis.

I also investigate communication-aware workload scheduling and nanopore basecalling workflows on heterogeneous computing systems, with an emphasis on scalability, resource use, and energy efficiency.
