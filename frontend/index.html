About

Research in algorithms and distributed computing

I am a Computer Science PhD scholar at the Indian Institute of Technology Jammu. My research interests are in parallel and distributed algorithms, with a focus on designing efficient, scalable, and theoretically sound methods for computation in distributed environments.

My work combines algorithmic analysis with practical implementation. I am interested in scalability, communication efficiency, complexity analysis, and reliable computation.

Research themes

Computing at scale, from signals to systems

Selected areas connecting algorithm design with high-performance and distributed computing.

01 / ALGORITHMS

Graph algorithms

Structure-aware methods for graph problems, including maximum-flow computation.

02 / SIGNALS

Nanopore signal processing

Performance-aware processing of nanopore sequencing and basecalling workflows.

03 / SYSTEMS

Distributed computing

Scalable execution, workload distribution, and communication-efficient systems.
